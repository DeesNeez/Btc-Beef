About

Our Mission

BTC Beef connects local customers directly with the farm, resulting in fewer middlemen, clear sourcing, and better value from pasture to plate.

  • Premium, carefully prepared cuts
  • Fair, transparent pricing
  • A stronger local food system

Every cut comes from carefully raised Red Angus cattle managed with regenerative, rotational grazing practices on our farm in Caledonia. Our peer-to-peer approach reflects the sound-money principles behind the BTC Beef name.

Why choose BTC Beef

Why Choose BTC Beef?
