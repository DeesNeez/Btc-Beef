Steak Recipes

Dependable steak recipes and make-ahead marinades.

Ribeye · 25 minutes

Cast-Iron Ribeye with Garlic Butter

A deeply browned crust, tender centre, and a quick thyme-garlic butter baste.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks (10–12 oz each)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp high-heat oil
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 garlic cloves (smashed)
  • 3 thyme sprigs

Method

  1. Pat dry and season.
  2. Sear in a very hot pan for 3–4 minutes per side.
  3. Add butter, garlic, and thyme; baste for 1 minute.
  4. Cook to at least 63°C (145°F) for medium-rare, then rest 7 minutes.

T-Bone · 30 minutes

Grilled T-Bone with Herb Salt

Simple seasoning and two-zone grilling let both sides of this bone-in steak cook evenly.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 T-bone steaks (12–14 oz each)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ tsp coarse salt
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tsp lemon zest

Method

  1. Mix salt, pepper, rosemary, and zest.
  2. Oil and season steaks.
  3. Sear over high heat, then finish over indirect heat, turning at least twice.
  4. Cook to at least 63°C (145°F) for medium-rare and rest 8 minutes.

Sirloin · 35 minutes

Peppercorn Sirloin with Pan Sauce

A bistro-style sirloin finished with shallots, cracked pepper, stock, and cream.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 sirloin steaks (8–10 oz each)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp cracked peppercorns
  • 1 medium shallot (minced)
  • ½ cup beef stock
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Method

  1. Season and sear steaks; cook to at least 63°C (145°F) for medium-rare.
  2. Rest steaks.
  3. Soften shallot in the same pan, add stock, and reduce by half.
  4. Stir in cream and butter; spoon over sliced steak.

Steak Marinades

Simple marinades with clear timing.

Marinade · 2–8 hours

Worcestershire–Montreal Steak Marinade

A versatile, all-cut blend of Worcestershire, garlic, lemon, and classic Montreal steak spice.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 steaks (8–12 oz each)
  • ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Montreal steak spice
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)

Method

  1. Whisk the Worcestershire sauce, olive oil, steak spice, lemon juice, and garlic in a bowl.
  2. Place the steaks in a sealable container or bag, pour over the marinade, and turn until evenly coated.
  3. Refrigerate for 2–8 hours, turning once halfway through if possible.
  4. Remove the steaks from the marinade, let the excess drip away, and discard the used marinade.
  5. Grill or pan-sear over high heat to your preferred doneness, using the temperature guide below. Rest for 5–8 minutes before slicing.

Marinade · 2–6 hours

Maple-Balsamic Herb Steak Marinade

A balanced sweet-and-savoury marinade that is especially nice with rib, T-bone, or sirloin steaks.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 steaks (8–12 oz each)
  • ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 tsp finely chopped fresh rosemary
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp kosher salt

Method

  1. Whisk the balsamic vinegar, olive oil, maple syrup, Dijon, garlic, rosemary, pepper, and salt until combined.
  2. Add the steaks and marinade to a sealable container or bag, turning the meat to coat every surface.
  3. Refrigerate for 2–6 hours.
  4. Remove the steaks, allow the excess marinade to drip away, and discard the used marinade.
  5. Grill or pan-sear over high heat to your preferred doneness. Rest for 5–8 minutes before serving.

Marinade · 1–4 hours

Soy-Ginger Steak Marinade

A fast, aromatic blend with a bright savoury finish, especially nice with sirloin tip or sirloin steaks.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 steaks (8–12 oz each)
  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp finely grated fresh ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 green onions (thinly sliced)

Method

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, brown sugar, ginger, and garlic until the sugar dissolves. Stir in the green onions.
  2. Combine the steaks and marinade in a sealable container or bag and turn to coat.
  3. Refrigerate for 1–4 hours.
  4. Remove the steaks, let the excess marinade drip away, and discard the used marinade.
  5. Grill or pan-sear over high heat to your preferred doneness. Rest for 5–8 minutes, then slice against the grain.

Trust the Temperature

A quick thermometer check takes the guesswork out and helps every cut land exactly where you like it. An overcooked steak is never a good time.

Medium-rare 55–57°C 130–134°F
Medium 58–62°C 135–144°F

Dutch Oven · 4 hours

Slow-Braised Beef Roast

Tender beef, root vegetables, herbs, and rich pan juices in one pot.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 beef roast (3–4 lb)
  • 2 medium onions (quartered)
  • 4 carrots (cut into chunks)
  • 4 garlic cloves (smashed)
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Brown the seasoned roast on all sides.
  2. Add vegetables, herbs, and stock.
  3. Cover and cook at 160°C (325°F) for 3–3½ hours.
  4. Rest 15 minutes and serve with pan juices.

Dutch Oven · 4¼ hours

Dutch-Oven Pot Roast with Red Wine

A deeply savoury chuck roast braised low and slow with red wine, herbs, carrots, and potatoes.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 well-marbled chuck roast (3–5 lb)
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 1 cup dry red wine
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 garlic cloves (smashed)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 rosemary sprigs
  • 3 thyme sprigs
  • 6 carrots
  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Season the roast and sear it thoroughly in a Dutch oven.
  2. Brown the onion, then deglaze the pot with red wine.
  3. Return the beef; add broth, Worcestershire, garlic, and herbs.
  4. Cover and braise at 135°C (275°F), adding the vegetables during cooking, until fork-tender—about 70 minutes per pound.

Based on State of Dinner’s recipe.

Dutch Oven · 3 hours

Beer-Braised Short Ribs

Fall-apart short ribs in a rich beer, beef-stock, tomato, and herb braising sauce.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 3–4 lb bone-in beef short ribs
  • 1 cup diced onion
  • 3 carrots (cut into chunks)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 12 oz beer
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 thyme sprigs
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Season and deeply brown the short ribs in a Dutch oven.
  2. Soften the onion and carrots; stir in tomato paste, garlic, and paprika.
  3. Deglaze with beer, then add stock, Worcestershire, herbs, and the ribs.
  4. Cover and braise at 165°C (325°F) for 2½–3 hours, until fork-tender.

Based on State of Dinner’s recipe.

Dutch Oven · 3½ hours

Smoky Beef Barbacoa

Juicy shredded chuck roast with chipotle, lime, cumin, and deeply browned edges.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 lb chuck roast
  • 1 large white onion (diced)
  • 5 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • ¼ cup lime juice
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 chipotle peppers plus 1 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp sea salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 20 small corn tortillas

Method

  1. Cut, season, and brown the chuck roast in batches.
  2. Soften onion and garlic in the same Dutch oven.
  3. Add the beef, stock, lime, vinegar, chipotle, and spices.
  4. Cover and braise at 175°C (350°F) for 2½–3 hours; shred and return to the cooking juices.

Based on The Modern Proper’s recipe.

Oven Roast · 2 hours

Herb-Crusted Roast Beef

Garlic, mustard, rosemary, and thyme form a savoury crust around a sliceable roast.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 tender beef roast (3–4 lb)
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 tbsp chopped rosemary
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Coat roast with oil, mustard, garlic, and herbs.
  2. Roast at 230°C (450°F) for 15 minutes.
  3. Reduce to 165°C (325°F) and cook to at least 63°C (145°F) for medium-rare.
  4. Rest 20 minutes before slicing thinly.

Soup · 1 hour

Beef & Barley Soup

A freezer-friendly soup with roast beef, barley, vegetables, and tomato-rich broth.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked roast beef (diced)
  • ¾ cup pearl barley
  • 1 medium onion (diced)
  • 2 carrots (diced)
  • 2 celery stalks (diced)
  • 1 can diced tomatoes (14 oz)
  • 6 cups beef stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Soften onion, carrots, and celery.

Shredded Beef Tacos

Fork-tender beef with smoky spices, lime, and enough cooking liquid to stay juicy.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 beef roast (3 lb)
  • 1 medium onion (diced)
  • 4 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

  • 12 small tortillas

Method

  1. Season and brown the roast.

Burgers · 20 minutes

Crispy-Edge Smash Burgers

Thin patties with deeply browned edges, melted cheddar, pickles, and sauce.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 4 cheddar slices
  • 4 burger buns
  • ½ cup sliced pickles
  • ½ small onion (thinly sliced)
  • ¼ cup burger sauce

Method

  1. Divide cold beef into 8 loose balls.
  2. Smash onto a hot griddle and season.
  3. Cook, flip, and continue until each patty reaches 71°C (160°F).
  4. Add cheese and stack two patties per bun.

Weeknight Beef Meatballs

Tender, savoury meatballs baked first and finished in tomato sauce.

View recipe

  • 1 large egg
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

One Pot · 50 minutes

Weeknight Beef Chili

A hearty tomato-and-bean chili with warm spices and flexible toppings.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion (diced)
  • 3 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can diced tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 1 can kidney beans (15 oz, drained)
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Heat a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it into small pieces, until well browned.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes. Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cumin, and tomato paste; cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the diced tomatoes and beef stock, scraping any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Stir in the kidney beans.
  4. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with cheddar, sour cream, green onion, cilantro, or sliced jalapeño if desired.

Stir-Fry · 25 minutes

Thai Basil Beef

A fast, savoury ground-beef stir-fry with sweet pepper, garlic, lime, chili, and plenty of fresh basil.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 red bell pepper (thinly sliced)
  • 1 medium sweet onion (thinly sliced)
  • 6 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp chili paste
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 cups cooked rice

Method

  1. Whisk the chili paste, soy sauce, fish sauce, brown sugar, and lime juice in a small bowl; set the sauce aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook for 8–10 minutes, breaking it into small pieces, until browned.
  3. Add the bell pepper, onion, and garlic. Stir-fry for about 5 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften but still have some bite.
  4. Pour in the sauce and add most of the basil. Cook for about 2 minutes, stirring, until the basil wilts and the sauce coats the beef.
  5. Serve over warm rice and finish with the reserved basil leaves.

Based on The Modern Proper’s recipe.

One Pan · 30 minutes

Creamy Beef & Egg Noodles

A quick, stroganoff-style skillet dinner with ground beef, tender noodles, and a tangy sour-cream sauce.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 12 oz egg noodles
  • ½ cup diced onion
  • 3 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 2 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. Cook the noodles until just tender and set aside.
  2. Soften onion and garlic, then brown the beef with the seasonings.
  3. Stir in flour, gradually add broth, and simmer until thickened.
  4. Remove from heat, stir in sour cream, and toss with the noodles.

Family Dinner · 40 minutes

Red Beef Enchiladas

Seasoned ground beef, melty cheese, and red enchilada sauce rolled into a bubbling family-style bake.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 1 medium yellow onion (diced)
  • 1 jalapeño (seeded and minced)
  • 3 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2½ cups shredded cheese (divided)
  • ¾ cup sour cream
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 10 small corn tortillas
  • 1 can red enchilada sauce (19 oz)
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Cook the beef, onion, jalapeño, and garlic in a large skillet over medium-high heat for about 8 minutes, breaking up the meat as it browns.
  2. Remove the skillet from the heat. Stir in 1½ cups of the cheese, the sour cream, and the taco seasoning.
  3. Wrap the tortillas in a damp towel and microwave for 1 minute so they roll without cracking. Warm the enchilada sauce separately.
  4. Lightly coat each tortilla with sauce, add about ¼ cup filling, roll tightly, and place seam-side down in a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
  5. Pour over the remaining sauce and sprinkle with the remaining cheese. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until bubbling and melted.
  6. Rest for 5 minutes, then finish with chopped cilantro before serving.

Based on The Modern Proper’s recipe.

Make Ahead · 1½ hours

Slow-Simmered Beef Bolognese

A rich, freezer-friendly pasta sauce with ground beef, vegetables, tomato, and milk.

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion (finely diced)
  • 1 large carrot (finely diced)
  • 2 celery stalks (finely diced)
  • 4 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • ½ cup whole milk
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Brown beef to 71°C (160°F).
  2. Add finely chopped vegetables and cook until soft.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, tomatoes, stock, and herbs.
  4. Simmer 60 minutes; finish with milk and serve over pasta.
